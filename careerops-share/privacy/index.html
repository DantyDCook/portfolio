Privacy Policy for CareerOps Share

Effective date: September 3, 2026

App: CareerOps Share

Android package: com.careerops.share

Developer: Danty Cook

CareerOps Share is an Android utility that helps users process job-posting links or text and prepare structured CareerOps requests for use with another application selected by the user.

Privacy summary: CareerOps Share v0.3.0 is designed to operate locally on the user's Android device. It does not operate a developer-controlled data collection server, require an account, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or use the Android INTERNET permission. The app processes text or links that the user explicitly shares with, pastes into, or enters into CareerOps Share.

1. Information CareerOps Share Processes

Job links and shared text. CareerOps Share may process information that you explicitly provide, including job-posting URLs, job descriptions or other shared text, source information derived from a URL such as LinkedIn or Indeed, job identifiers contained in supported URLs, canonicalized job URLs, and CareerOps request content generated from the information you provide.

This processing occurs locally on your device. CareerOps Share v0.3.0 does not independently obtain job information from websites or external servers.

App preferences. CareerOps Share stores certain settings locally on your device so the app can remember your configuration. These may include the selected CareerOps action, destination, request format or profile, model-preference metadata, regular-share defaults, Quick Share or Direct Share slot configuration, automatic-forwarding preferences, and appearance or theme settings.

These preferences are stored using Android app-private storage.

2. Information We Collect

CareerOps Share v0.3.0 does not transmit user data to a server operated by the developer and does not collect user information into a developer-controlled database.

The developer does not receive your job-posting content, CareerOps request content, local preferences, or usage activity merely because you use CareerOps Share.

3. Sending Information to Other Apps

A core function of CareerOps Share is allowing you to send a prepared CareerOps request to another Android application.

Depending on your configuration, this may include ChatGPT or another application you select through the Android system chooser. CareerOps Share transfers this information only as part of a user-selected or user-configured sharing action.

Once information is delivered to another application, that application's own terms, privacy policy, data collection practices, and security practices apply. CareerOps Share does not control how a receiving application processes, stores, transmits, or uses information after transfer.

4. Network Access

CareerOps Share v0.3.0 does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not directly send CareerOps requests to a CareerOps server, AI provider, analytics provider, advertising network, or other remote service.

Future versions may add optional network-connected functionality. If that occurs, this Privacy Policy and applicable in-app disclosures will be updated to reflect those capabilities.

5. Local Storage and Retention

Job-posting text and URLs are used to create and display the current CareerOps request. CareerOps Share v0.3.0 does not intentionally maintain a server-side or developer-controlled history of this information.

Persistent app preferences remain on the device until you change or clear the applicable setting, clear CareerOps Share's app data through Android, uninstall the app, or Android otherwise removes the app's stored data.

Android may back up eligible app preferences according to your device and platform backup settings. Any such platform backup is controlled by the Android device or backup provider rather than by CareerOps Share.

6. Deleting Your Data

CareerOps Share does not maintain a server-side user account or developer-controlled user database in v0.3.0.

You can remove locally stored CareerOps Share information by changing or clearing settings within the app where available, using Android's Clear storage or Clear app data function, or uninstalling CareerOps Share.

Platform backup copies, if enabled on your device, are governed by the applicable Android or backup-provider settings. Because CareerOps Share does not maintain a developer-controlled account or remote user-data store in v0.3.0, there is no separate server-side account-deletion process.

7. Security

CareerOps Share minimizes data exposure by processing its current functionality locally and by not operating a network connection of its own in v0.3.0.

Persistent preferences are stored within Android's app-private storage and are protected by the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system. When you intentionally transfer information to another application, the security of that information after transfer is governed by the receiving application and any services it uses.

No method of electronic storage or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

CareerOps Share v0.3.0 does not include advertising networks, behavioral advertising, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking SDKs, advertising-ID collection, or developer-operated telemetry.

CareerOps Share does not sell or monetize user data.

9. Third-Party Services

CareerOps Share may interact with another installed Android application only when you choose or configure that application as a destination.

Third-party applications and services are independent from CareerOps Share. Their privacy policies govern information they receive. CareerOps Share is not affiliated with or responsible for the privacy practices of third-party applications or services solely because they can receive content through Android's sharing system.

10. Children's Privacy

CareerOps Share is intended for career, employment, and job-application workflows and is not designed or directed toward children under 13.

CareerOps Share does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CareerOps Share's functionality or data-handling practices change. Material changes to data collection, transmission, storage, third-party integrations, or network functionality will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy and, where required, appropriate in-app disclosures.

The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version of the policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns regarding CareerOps Share, contact the developer at DantyDCook+portfolio@gmail.com.
